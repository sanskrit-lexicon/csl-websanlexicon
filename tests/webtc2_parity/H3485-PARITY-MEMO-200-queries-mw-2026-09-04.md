## Sample

generated by [make_queries200.py](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-websanlexicon/blob/main/tests/webtc2_parity/make_queries200.py) (fixed strides over the
dump, no RNG, committed for reproducibility): the H3633 31-query matrix

exact/prefix/suffix/instring/substring × hwonly/both/textonly, wildcard
shapes (`kr*`, `*tva`, `k*a`, `dev?`, `ama|eva`, pure `*`, `?`), HK
transliteration inputs, English exact/prefix/suffix/instring/substring incl.
uppercase and zero-match terms, `max=5` and `max=100` clamps. The harness
([run_parity.php](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-websanlexicon/blob/main/tests/webtc2_parity/run_parity.php)) auto-follows advertised `lastLnum` with a
page-2 request, so pagination parity is covered per query.

## Verdict

The merged #153 index path meets H3485's goal condition: the webtc2 query
path uses the generation-time index when present, returns byte-equal result
sets vs the legacy flat-file scan on a 200-query sample, and falls back
inert-safely (fallback and stale-guard phases keep legacy timing — no
acceleration, no divergence, no exception paths).
